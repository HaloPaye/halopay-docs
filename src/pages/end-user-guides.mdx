# End-User Guides

This guide explains how merchants use the HaloPay POS terminal in daily operations.

## 1. Onboarding and KYC
Merchants complete KYC before receiving payments. This process usually clears within 24 hours.
1. Open the HaloPay app.
2. Go to **Merchant Settings**.
3. Upload front and back photos of a valid Government ID.
4. Take a clear selfie.
5. Submit for compliance review.

## 2. Receiving Offline Payments
You can accept payments without internet access.
1. Enter the fiat amount on the POS keypad.
2. The POS calculates the USDC amount using a cached exchange rate.
3. The app displays a SEP-0007 QR code.
4. The buyer scans the QR code with a Stellar wallet (e.g. Vibrant) and signs the transaction.

## 3. End-of-Day Settlement
When you reconnect to the internet, the POS processes settlements.
1. The POS syncs offline logs to the backend.
2. The backend converts USDC balances through a SEP-24 off-ramp.
3. Funds arrive in your local bank account or mobile wallet. Transfer times range from 1 to 3 hours depending on the local anchor.
