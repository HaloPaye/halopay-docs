# Introduction

Welcome to HaloPay documentation.

HaloPay is an offline-first Point-of-Sale (POS) and settlement protocol on the Stellar network. It enables merchants in crisis zones to accept digital humanitarian aid (like USDC) and settle it into local fiat. In 2023, 1.4 billion adults remained unbanked. HaloPay targets this demographic by bridging digital assets and cash.

## Why HaloPay?
- **Offline-First**: Generate payment QR codes without an internet connection. The system caches exchange rates for up to 48 hours.
- **Stellar Native**: Built directly on Stellar. It uses SEP-10, SEP-12, and SEP-24 standards.
- **Instant Settlement**: Off-ramp transactions process in under 5 seconds. Fees average $0.00001 per transaction.
